# Analysis Pack v1 - Customer Cohort Retention (Trusted Dates)

## Objective
Extend Analysis Pack v1 with a trusted-dates customer cohort retention view that measures how customer groups behave over time after their first completed order.

This phase builds on the existing customer behavior and customer recency work, and adds the first explicit retention framework to the warehouse.

## Business goal
The purpose of this work is to answer questions such as:
- after customers place their first order, how often do they return in later months?
- are newer customer cohorts retaining better or worse than older cohorts?
- how quickly do cohorts decay after acquisition?
- how much revenue is generated by cohorts over time?
- is the business benefiting from retention, or relying mostly on new customer acquisition?

This is a natural next step after customer behavior and recency analysis because it shifts the project from static customer summaries toward time-based lifecycle analysis.

---

## Model design

### Grain
One row per `cohort_month` x `months_since_first_order`.

### Source of truth
The model is built from `marts.fct_orders`, using customer email as the practical customer key and completed orders as the basis for cohort assignment and later activity.

### Trusted-dates logic
This view uses only orders with trusted historical timing.

Orders flagged with `is_suspect_historical_timing = TRUE` are excluded. This is important because cohort analysis depends directly on the timing of a customer’s first order. If that date is wrong, the customer is assigned to the wrong cohort and the retention output becomes structurally misleading.

### Key outputs
The model includes:
- `cohort_month`
- `months_since_first_order`
- `customers_in_cohort`
- `customers_ordering_in_period`
- `retention_rate`
- `period_orders`
- `period_revenue`

---

## Important modeling choices

### Cohort definition
A customer’s cohort is defined as the month of their first completed order with trusted timing.

### Completed-order logic
Cancelled and voided orders are excluded. This keeps cohort assignment and retention behavior tied to actual completed customer purchasing.

### Retention logic
Retention is measured as:

`customers_ordering_in_period / customers_in_cohort`

This creates a standard cohort retention framework where:
- month 0 should always equal 100 percent retention
- later months show what share of the original cohort returned to order again

### Partial January 2021 cohort
Because trusted-date filtering excludes suspect historical timing records, the January 2021 cohort becomes a small partial month rather than the artificially inflated cohort seen in untrusted date logic.

This is expected and preferable to including structurally unreliable dates.

## Validation performed

### Structural checks
- validated one row per `cohort_month` x `months_since_first_order`
- duplicate grain check returned 0 rows

### Cohort sanity checks
- month 0 customer counts matched cohort size
- month 0 retention rate was 1.0000 for all cohorts
- no retention rates exceeded 1.0000

### Tie-out checks
- total cohort customers tied out to distinct trusted completed-order customers in `fct_orders`

### Business sanity checks
- reviewed the full cohort retention table
- reviewed month 0 cohort sanity
- reviewed month 1 retention by cohort
- reviewed average retention by lifecycle month
